How this is worked out
Net savings = running balance (past contributions minus any penalties already charged) + this period's contribution − current outstanding loan due.
Capital % = a member's net savings ÷ everyone's net savings combined. Members with an unpaid loan still earn ROI on their capital.
Total income = total interest earned on loans + penalties collected this period.
Distributable income = 75% (adjustable in Settings) of total income, split by capital % — this is each member's ROI.
Payout = net savings + that member's ROI share. Closing a period locks these numbers into History and starts a fresh one.
